The research finding

Most safety leadership programs
measure safety work —
not the safety of work.

Visits are counted. Actions are closed. Conditions go unchanged. The program looks active while the risk stays exactly where it was.

Activity
Visits completed, observations logged, actions closed.
Outcome
Conditions unchanged. Risk unreduced.
The gap
No mechanism to convert field insight into system change.
02
Why programs plateau

Four traps that keep the loop from closing.

TRAP 01
Scripted engagement
Generic checklists and scripted visits make the field conversation symbolic, not real.
TRAP 02
Siloed learning
A weak signal on one site never reaches the team managing the next. Learning stays local.
TRAP 03
Activity metrics
If the measure is visits and closures, the program optimises for visits and closures — not change.
TRAP 04
Unresolved trade-offs
Production pressure versus safety goes untracked. Leaders manage it locally. The pattern stays invisible.
03
What the research prescribes

Stop counting.
Start converting.

Field insight needs a mechanism to become operational change — at the system level, not just the site level. The program’s job is to make that conversion reliable.

Practice
Are leaders engaging with real work, or performing a visit?
Response
How quickly does a signal become an action? Does it hold?
Improvement
Are conditions actually changing? Is the same risk recurring?
