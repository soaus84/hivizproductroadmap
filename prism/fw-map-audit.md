# Forge Works Map audit: factors, levels and indicators

**For review** · 27 September 2026 · Source: *The Forge Works blueprint for improving the safety of work* (Forge Works Map®, © 2020), `temp-references/Blueprint unlocked for copying (1).pdf`

This audit checks the Map in the Hiviz concept (`prism/concept/index.html`) and the platform spec (`specs/globals/fw-map-blueprint.md`) against the Blueprint. It looks at three things:

1. whether factor names, questions and level descriptions match the Blueprint
2. which **indicators** (threads) run through each factor's three level descriptions, and where a level is silent on a thread
3. how the three draft indicators per factor in the concept compare

Everything under "Proposed indicators" is a recommendation for Forge Works to confirm. The Blueprint doesn't list indicators itself. They're read out of the level descriptions.

---

## 1. Summary

| Finding | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Each factor has named levels** | The Blueprint's summary table (p15) names every level per factor, e.g. Senior leadership is *Transactional → Transformational → Servant leadership*. The concept only shows the generic Compliant / Leading / Resilient. The named levels should be shown alongside. |
| **Questions and level text have drifted** | The concept paraphrases the questions and level descriptions, and the spec paraphrases them further. Some spec lines aren't in the Blueprint at all, e.g. *"Handover procedures exist"* (communication), *"atrophy scores"* (monitoring), *"Deviation is treated as a disciplinary issue"* (frontline workers). Recommend using the Blueprint wording. |
| **Indicators are real but uneven** | Every factor has 4–6 recurring threads in its descriptions. Most run through all three levels. Some appear at only one or two levels (marked **–** below). Those gaps need a line authoring by Forge Works so the indicator can be rated at every level. |
| **About half the concept's drafts are off** | Of the 45 draft indicators: **10 match** a Blueprint thread, **22 partly match**, and **13 aren't in the Blueprint**. The biggest miss is Communication and coordination: the concept's headline indicator, *"Shift and crew handovers"*, isn't in the Blueprint. Handovers are evidence for its *team coordination* and *information flow* threads. |
| **Names differ inside the Blueprint** | *Safety organisation* (table) vs *Safety professionals* (factor page) · *Frontline workers* vs *Frontline workforce* · *Communication and coordination* vs *Communications & coordination* · *Contractor management* vs *Contract management* · *Resource allocation* vs *Resource allocation to reduce risk*. Forge Works should confirm the canonical names. |
| **Not every thread can be seen in records** | Some threads show up directly in the feed Prism reads (pre-starts, permits, inspection quality, the language used in investigations). Others need management input such as Focus reviews, investigations, surveys or interviews. The Blueprint's own case study used 100+ interviews. Marking the evidence source per indicator keeps the scoring honest. |

**Proposed set:** 71 indicators across the 15 factors (4–5 each). **58** are described at all three levels. **13** need one or two level descriptions authored, plus one optional sixth indicator for Contractor management.

**Key to the tables below**
- **C / L / R**: ✓ if the Blueprint's Compliant, Leading or Resilient description covers the thread. **–** if it's silent, so a line needs authoring.
- **Draft**: how the concept's draft indicator compares. **✓** match, **~** partial, **✗** not in the Blueprint, blank if there's no draft for it.
- **Evidence** (where Prism can get it): **Rec** = enriched records, **Ins** = insights and investigations, **Mgmt** = management input (Focus reviews, surveys, interviews).

1. **Show the named levels.** Show each factor's own level names (e.g. *Two-way communication*) next to Compliant, Leading and Resilient.
2. **Use the Blueprint's questions and level text**, verbatim or as faithful short summaries, in place of my paraphrases. Update `specs/globals/fw-map-blueprint.md` to match.
3. **Replace the 45 draft indicators** with the 71 proposed. Indicators with a missing level show "not yet described" at that level.
4. **Re-map the example insights:**

|---|---|---|
| Controls failing at handovers | Communication and coordination | Team coordination |

## 6. Questions for Forge Works

1. Which factor names are canonical, given the differences inside the Blueprint (section 1)?
2. Can the proposed indicators be used as-is, or does Forge Works hold its own indicator set from diagnostic work, such as the interview protocol used with Tronox?
3. Should factor ratings sit at whole levels only, or can a factor be "between" levels (e.g. mostly Compliant with some Leading)?
4. Is it acceptable for Hiviz to show the level descriptions verbatim, given the Blueprint is © Forge Works and was released freely?

## 7. Decisions (27 Sep 2026)

1. **Factor names.** Use Safety organisation, Frontline workforce, Communication & coordination, Contractor management and Resource allocation.
2. **Purpose of indicators.** Indicators are after the fact. Prism uses them to map source records and insights to a factor, and to give recommendations, reports and insight text the Map's language (where you are, and what the next level looks like). The concept now shows the factor and indicator on every insight (an "On the Forge Works Map" card) and in the record drawer.
3. **Mixed maturity.** A factor can sit between levels, and that is expected. Ratings are continuous (for example "Compliant, some Leading"), and the level bar fills part-way.
4. **Level text.** The Blueprint is the reference, not a script. Level summaries in the concept are faithful paraphrases. In the product, Prism writes them for the situation, keeping them accurate as diagnostic descriptions rather than verbatim.

Also applied in the concept:
- Each factor shows its named levels.
- The 71 proposed indicators replace the draft 45, each with its evidence source (Records, Insights, Management input).
- Indicators that only management can evidence are shown as "Needs management input", not scored from records.
- Each indicator expands to show what it looks like at Compliant, Leading and Resilient. Gaps show as "Not yet described in the Forge Works Map".
- Insights were re-mapped as in section 5.
