2 · Where your voice lives

Every Prism job reads with three layers. The middle one is you.

Each job, from critical risk to senior leadership, carries these layers every time it runs. The middle one is what no other product can copy.

1

The Forge Works method

The shared language: the Forge Works Map® Blueprint with its 15 factors and three maturity levels, and the definitions of signal, energy and barrier. This is what keeps every reading consistent.

2

The Forge Works voice

What Forge Works consultants look for, won’t accept and would recommend, written down for each safety domain and led by David Provan and Ivica Ninic. Prism reads every record against it, and recommends the way you would.

  • “Stop judging a pre-start by whether it’s signed. Watch whether it changes how the job starts.”David Provan
  • “Report what could have killed someone alongside the injuries. Ask what stopped each one, and whether it will next time.”Ivica Ninic
  • “Keep changing the work, not the reminders. A spotter on the roster is a spotter who’s there.”David Provan
  • “Plan for every control that comes off for the work. Name who puts it back, and when, before the job starts.”Ivica Ninic
3

The client’s own context

Their critical control register and management system, when they connect them, so a reading can say which of their own controls or procedures was involved.
