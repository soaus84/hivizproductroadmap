The intelligence layer for the safety system you already own.

Hiviz connects to the incident, inspection and hazard tools an organisation already runs. Prism, its AI layer, turns their records into insight, and Hiviz turns that into decisions, plans and proof of what worked. There's nothing to migrate and no new forms to fill in. How it works

Four steps from their data to a manager's desk

01 · Connect

Plug into what's there

Webhooks, API polling, platform tables, or a CSV drop. Read-only by default. We backfill the last 12 months, then stay live.

New: connector layer, schema and site mapping
02 · Refract

Enrich each record

Prism gives every incident, hazard, failed inspection item and investigation a signal type, energy, barrier state, key hazard and FW Map® hint, each with a reason.

Reuses the observation enrichment job and taxonomies
03 · Pool

Build an opinionated data pool

Records from every tool land in one classified pool. Nightly runs look for trends by site, region and work type. Serious single events skip the queue.

Reuses trend detection and direct-path rules
04 · Surface

Tell managers what it means

Critical Insights for review, weekly digests, monthly reports, and visit briefings. Suggested actions go back into the customer's own tool.

Reuses insight, reporting, briefs, visits
The closed loop

Done isn't the same as worked. Hiviz shows both.

Most tools stop when the action is ticked off. Hiviz follows every insight until the pattern has actually changed, or proves that it hasn't.

01 · Raised

Prism finds the pattern

Across tools and sites, with a reason for every call. Serious single events skip the queue.

02 · Owned

The right manager takes it

The line manager responsible owns it. If it needs more than their level can give, it escalates up the purview.

03 · Planned

One to three actions

Hiviz recommends them from Forge Works guidance. The owner approves the plan, and who approved it is recorded.

04 · Worked on

Checked in, and listened to

Action owners check in before each meeting. Prism listens for the change the plan expects: working, too early, or pattern continues.

05 · Proven

A 90-day retro

Did the pattern stop, and stay stopped? Worked, partly, didn't, or can't tell. The room confirms or challenges Prism's verdict.

Every retro adds to a record of what works in this organisation, and what doesn't, so the next plan starts from evidence. See an insight in the concept →

Rituals

Meetings that run themselves, and never lose an item

Three management rituals share one stepped, full-screen format, built for a projector or a call: who's here, check-ins, decisions, summary. Run one and you can run them all.

Fortnightly

Focus review

Managers of a purview check in on their actions, then decide the insights, grouped by what each one needs: an owner, a plan, a retro to confirm.

Monthly

Strategy progress

Owners check in the week before. The room only discusses what needs it: where the owner and Prism disagree, and who needs help.

Quarterly

Planning

Leaders set the year's objectives on Map factors: keep, adjust or retire each one, and choose any new ones. Three at most.

  • Every item needs a decision, or is carried forward on purpose.
  • Missed check-ins are recorded, not hidden.
  • Ending early is a formal close. The next meeting starts with what's left.
  • One plain question for the room on every screen.

Maturity, measured

The Forge Works Map®, scored every week from four kinds of evidence

Maturity stops being a survey every two years. Hiviz scores it continuously, names what it can't see, and counts the lack of evidence as well as the evidence.

Records

What happens on site, from every tool, enriched by Prism.

Insights

Patterns across tools and sites, and what the organisation did about them.

Management poll

One or two anonymous questions a fortnight in My Focus, for what only people can tell. Where managers split, the room hears why.

Hiviz itself

What people actually do: who approves plans, whether leaders turn up, what triggers spending. Evidence of maturity, or of its lack.

Strategy sits on top. Objectives are set on Map factors, and key results are scored from real records, so progress shows every week, not once a quarter. See the Map in the concept →
