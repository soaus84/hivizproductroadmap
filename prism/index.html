The intelligence layer for the safety system you already own.

Prism connects to the incident, inspection and hazard tools an organisation already runs and turns their records into insight for managers. There's nothing to migrate and no new forms to fill in. The TV stays; we make it smart.

Why this, why now

A leaner product for a tighter market

Consulting is thinning out

AI is taking the advisory work that has funded Forge Works. We need recurring revenue that doesn't depend on a consultant being in the room.

Nobody wants another system of record

Organisations have already bought an EHS platform and trained people on it. Asking them to replace it means a year of change management we can't fund.

Managers have records, not reads

The data exists. What's missing is someone telling a manager what it means this week, and why. That's the part Hiviz already does well.

How it works

Four steps from their data to a manager's desk

01 · Connect

Plug into what's there

Webhooks, API polling, platform tables, or a CSV drop. Read-only by default. We backfill the last 12 months, then stay live.

New: connector layer, schema and site mapping
02 · Refract

Enrich each record

Every incident, hazard, failed inspection item and investigation gets a signal type, energy, barrier state, key hazard and FW Map® hint, each with a reason.

Reuses the observation enrichment job and taxonomies
03 · Pool

Build an opinionated data pool

Records from every tool land in one classified pool. Nightly runs look for trends by site, region and work type. Serious single events skip the queue.

Reuses trend detection and direct-path rules
04 · Surface

Tell managers what it means

Critical Insights for review, weekly digests, monthly reports, and visit briefings. Suggested actions go back into the customer's own tool.

Reuses insight, reporting, briefs, visits
What a manager gets

Only manager surfaces. Nothing for crew.

  • Critical InsightsPattern-level findings drafted by AI, reviewed and owned by a safety manager.
  • Periodic intelligence reportsWeekly digest and monthly narrative, scoped to the manager's region or division.
  • Field leadership visitsA briefing before each site visit, with observation capture on the walk. These are the only records created in Hiviz.
  • Systemic pictureFW Map® capacity profile, blind spots, and sites that have gone quiet in their own system.
  • Action pushSuggested actions written back into the tool the site already uses, where its API allows.
