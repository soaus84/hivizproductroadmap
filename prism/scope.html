How data comes in, gets refracted, and becomes insight

Prism reads records from the customer's tools, enriches them with the Hiviz stack, pools them, and runs scheduled and direct-path detection. Only managers see the output. Stages outlined in gold are new build; everything else reuses a Hiviz spec.

Cadence

When insight is generated

Serious single events move in minutes. Patterns come out of scheduled runs, so managers get a steady rhythm instead of a stream of alerts.

On enrichment

Direct path

Barrier failure or energy event at ≥ 0.70, or a serious imported incident. An insight draft is ready in minutes.

Nightly

Trend detection

Thresholds per org level and work type across the enriched pool. Rates are used where exposure data exists.

Weekly

Systemic roll-up

FW capacity profile, blind spots, and atrophy. Shows sites that have gone quiet in their own system.

Weekly

Manager digest

What changed, what's waiting for review, and which pushed actions are overdue.

Monthly · quarterly

Intelligence report

A narrative report per region or division, built from the existing reporting modules plus new source-coverage modules.

Source types

What to capture from their current reporting

Observations and incidents are the core. The other types either add evidence to the pool or give the pool context: activity, exposure, and control health. Exposure hours matter most: without them every trend is a raw count.

Thin entities

What Prism stores, and what it doesn't

Prism keeps a pointer, a fingerprint, the enrichment, and a short scrubbed summary. Raw record bodies, attachments and identifying fields stay in the customer's system.

new

  • vendor, pattern
  • auth_ref · scopes

Specs to write next

  1. Connectors: contract for each pattern, retry and dedupe rules, rate-limit budgets, health states.
  2. Schema mapping: the connector.map_schema prompt, FieldMap versioning, and the confirmation screen.
  3. Source enrichment: user-prompt variants for incident, inspection finding, investigation, and control verification. The system prompt stays in OBSERVATION-CAPTURE.md.
  4. Action push: write-back contract per vendor and the fallback tracker.
  5. Data quality report module: coverage by site, unmapped values, low-confidence share, sites with no records. This is also a strong sales artefact.
  6. First light pilot kit: export templates per vendor, intake checklist, and a report template.
  7. Prism sim: paste a CSV, watch records refract, see a first insight drafted.
