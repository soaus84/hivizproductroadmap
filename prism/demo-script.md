# Hiviz manager concept: demo script

About 15 minutes. Start at `prism/concept/index.html`, signed in as the Infrastructure division manager. Refreshing the page resets the demo data.

**The one line to leave them with:** Hiviz turns what managers see in the field, and what their systems already record, into a few clear things to act on. It closes the loop from insight to plan to "did it work?"

---

## 1. Insights: what needs attention right now (3 min)

- **Open on:** Insights, Infrastructure division.
- **Say:** "This is the story right now, written by Prism from every record across the division. Not a dashboard: a few insights, grouped by what they need."
- **Click:** *Suspended loads are moving outside the planned path*.
  - **What each source says:** Procore and Mitti show the pattern. **Hiviz visits** show the why: crews said rope goes on as a tag line when the rigging box is empty. That's what systems don't capture.
  - **The plan:** 1–3 actions, an owner who approves, a check-in date, and a 90-day retro that asks "did it work?", not just "was it done?".

## 2. Sources: Hiviz works on its own (2 min)

- **Click:** Sources.
- **Say:** "**Built into Hiviz** comes first: leadership visits and observations from the Hiviz app. On their own they're enough for Prism to find patterns and score the Map. Connected systems add depth, but you don't need them on day one."
- **Proof:** *Spotters for reversing plant* (back in Insights) is built entirely from Hiviz's own records: observations plus visit conversations.
- **Optional:** point at Mitti *Failing*. Hiviz tells you when the picture is incomplete, and what it's doing about it.

## 3. Sites and My Focus: field leadership (2 min)

- **Click:** Sites. Quarry Link has *gone quiet*: no reports for 19 days, not visited in 41.
- **Click:** My Focus. Everything that needs *you*, from retros to confirm and actions to check in on, to a site to visit and one anonymous poll question. Items clear when they're done.

## 4. The Map: how mature are we, and where to focus (3 min)

- **Click:** Map. Forge Works factors scored from evidence, not a survey. "Where to focus" picks the clearest gap, objectives off track, and anything worsening.
- **Click:** one factor. Show the indicators, the **management poll** (anonymous, by level), and **Hiviz evidence**, which is what the platform itself records about how leaders behave.
- **Use the purview picker:** switch to Building division, then **Back to Infrastructure**. The Map follows where you're looking.

## 5. Strategy: one strategy, the same everywhere (2 min)

- **Click:** Strategy. "Set once for the whole company at quarterly planning. It reads the same in every purview; your area's objectives are tagged."
- **Switch:** Report to **Tree**. Goal → objectives → key results → programs → actions. Done actions stay visible, because progress shouldn't hide scope.
- **In planning week:** candidates for next quarter appear with the insights that make the case for them.

## 6. Run a Focus review (3 min)

- **Back to:** Insights, then **Start a Focus review**. It goes full screen, built to put on a projector.
- **Who's here** → **Check-ins**, where owners updated before the meeting so it only discusses what's stuck → **Decide**, where every item needs a decision → **Summary**.
- **Show:** **End meeting**. Anything not reached carries forward to the next review. Nothing is lost.

---

## If they ask

- **"What if we have no other systems?"** Section 2: Hiviz visits and observations are enough to start.
- **"Who sees what?"** Everyone has a home purview and can look across. Strategy is company-wide; insights, sites and the Map follow where you're looking.
- **"How do you know it's working?"** The 90-day retro, plus key results moving on the Map.

**Agreed but not built yet:** divisions drafting their own programs against key results, choosing key results at planning, company and division actions, and an icon pass.
